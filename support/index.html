Support

Most questions are answered below. If yours isn’t, write to us.

Writing to us

Tell us what you were doing and what happened instead. It helps to include:

  • Your iPhone model and iOS version.
  • The HeyMile version, shown at the bottom of Settings in the app.
  • For a tracking problem, the day and rough time of the drive.
  • A screenshot, if it shows the problem.

We cannot see your log, so we may ask you to describe a trip. Please do not send passwords or payment details.

Tracking

My drives aren’t being logged

  • Open HeyMile and look at the top of the first screen. It should say Tracking on. If it says anything else, tap it.
  • In the iPhone’s Settings, open HeyMile, then Location, and choose Always, with Precise Location on.
  • Low Power Mode can delay detection, so a drive may start a little late.
  • For the most reliable start, tap the tracking status in HeyMile and set up Start when your car connects.

A drive is missing or wrong

Tell HeyMile where you drove, for example “From 12 Main St to 400 Oak Ave”. It measures the distance with Apple Maps and asks you to confirm.

To fix a trip, say what to change, for example “Mark the trip to Main St as personal” or “Delete my last trip”. HeyMile asks before it changes anything, and a deleted trip stays in Recently Deleted for 30 days. You can also open any trip from Trips and edit it.

The drive doesn’t show on my Lock Screen

iOS shows a drive on the Lock Screen when it starts while HeyMile is open, or when it starts from the car connection. The first time, iOS asks “Allow Live Activities from HeyMile?”: tap Allow. You can also turn them on in the iPhone’s Settings, under Apps, then HeyMile. Drives are logged either way.

Talking to HeyMile

What can I say?

Type or tap the microphone and say it the way you would to a person:

  • Log a trip: “From the office to 1200 Main St”. A place’s name works too, like “From home to the mall on Main Street”: HeyMile finds it in Maps and asks if it’s the right one.
  • Ask: “How many miles this month?”, “What was my last trip?”, “What trips did I take Monday?”.
  • Fix a trip: “Make Monday’s trip personal”, “Delete my last trip”, “Undo”.
  • Drive: “Start a drive”, “End the drive”.
  • See your log: “Show my trips”, “Send my report”.
  • Change your mind: “Never mind”.

HeyMile keeps to your mileage log. It asks before it deletes or changes anything, and it never adds a trip without your yes.

How do I clear the conversation?

Swipe it sideways, or tap Clear above it. Anything left half-done is dropped, and nothing is saved. Leaving HeyMile and coming back also starts with a clean screen.

Can I use HeyMile in Spanish?

Yes. HeyMile follows your iPhone’s language, typed or spoken. To use Spanish for HeyMile only, open the iPhone’s Settings, then Apps, HeyMile, Language.

Widget, controls and CarPlay

How do I add the widget?

Touch and hold the Home Screen, tap Edit, then Add Widget, and choose HeyMile. When a drive is waiting, the widget shows Business and Personal: one tap answers.

How do I add the Drive control?

Open Control Center, tap +, then Add a Control, and choose HeyMile, then Drive. One press starts a drive, another ends it. To put it on the Action button, open the iPhone’s Settings, then Action Button, and choose Controls.

Where is HeyMile in CarPlay?

HeyMile appears on your car’s screen with the other CarPlay apps. While you drive, it shows the miles with two buttons, Business and Personal. For the question to appear on the car’s screen when you park, turn on Show in CarPlay in the iPhone’s Settings, under Notifications, then HeyMile.

Reports

How do I get my report?

Open Reports, choose the month or year, then View report or Share. If your subscription has ended, you can still open and export your reports.

Subscription

How do I cancel?

In HeyMile, open Settings and choose Manage subscription. You can also cancel in the iPhone’s Settings under your name, then Subscriptions.

How do I get a refund?

Purchases are billed by Apple, and Apple handles refunds. Ask at reportaproblem.apple.com.

I subscribed but the app says I haven’t

In HeyMile, open Settings and choose Restore purchases. Make sure the iPhone is signed in to the Apple Account you subscribed with.

I have a code

In HeyMile, open Settings and choose Redeem a code.

Your data

I got a new iPhone

Sign in to the same Apple Account with iCloud turned on, install HeyMile, and your log returns.

I deleted HeyMile and installed it again

Your log returns from iCloud shortly after you open HeyMile, as long as iCloud backup said Backed up before the app was deleted. You can check it at the top of Settings in HeyMile at any time.

I deleted a trip by mistake

Open Trips and scroll to Recently Deleted. Deleted trips stay there for 30 days. Swipe one to the right to restore it.

How do I delete my data?

In HeyMile, open Settings and choose Delete all my data. HeyMile has no accounts, so there is no account to delete. The record that you accepted the terms is kept, as the Privacy Policy explains.

Where is my data kept?

Your log is on your iPhone and in your own iCloud. The Privacy Policy explains it in full.
