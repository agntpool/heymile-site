Questions.

How much does HeyMile cost?
$0.99 a month, and the first month is free for new subscribers. It renews each month until you cancel, billed through your Apple Account. If you cancel, you can still read and export your records.
Does HeyMile log drives automatically?
Yes, once two things are on: location access set to Always, and tracking turned on. Then HeyMile detects your drives and logs the miles, even when the app is closed. You can also have every drive start when your car connects. When you arrive, the Lock Screen asks “Business or personal?” and one tap answers.
What do I need to turn on?
In the iPhone’s Settings, tap Apps, then HeyMile, then Location, and choose Always, with Precise Location on. When you turn tracking on, iOS asks twice: allow location access, then choose Change to Always Allow. With While Using the App only, HeyMile can’t log drives on its own. Then check the top of HeyMile’s first screen: it should say Tracking on. If it says anything else, tap it.
Does HeyMile work with CarPlay?
Yes. On the car’s screen HeyMile shows the drive’s miles while you drive. When the drive ends, two big buttons, Business and Personal, let you answer with one tap before you leave the car.
Do I need CarPlay to use HeyMile?
No. CarPlay is optional. Without it, the iPhone’s Lock Screen and the Home Screen widget ask “Business or personal?” after each drive, and one tap answers. CarPlay adds two big buttons on the car’s screen.
What is in the report?
Every trip with its date, route, purpose and miles, and your odometer readings, exported as a PDF or a spreadsheet for the month or the year.
Do I need an account?
No. HeyMile has no account, no ads and no analytics. Your log lives on your iPhone and in your own iCloud.
Which iPhones does HeyMile run on?
iPhone with iOS 26 or later, in English or Spanish.
Is HeyMile free?
HeyMile is free to download. Using it requires the HeyMile Monthly subscription: $0.99 a month, with the first month free for new subscribers.
Does HeyMile work if I drive for more than one app?
Yes. HeyMile doesn’t connect to rideshare, delivery or booking apps. It logs the drives your iPhone detects, so miles from every app you drive for end up in one log.
Does HeyMile drain my battery?
Between drives HeyMile relies on low-power signals from iOS. Precise GPS runs only while a drive is being recorded.
Does HeyMile work on Android?
No. HeyMile is an iPhone app, for iOS 26 or later, and it isn’t available for Android, iPad or the web.
Is HeyMile tax advice?
No. HeyMile is a record-keeping tool. Dollar figures are estimates based on your log and the IRS standard mileage rate and aren’t guaranteed. Confirm with your tax advisor before you file.
