A mileage log on your car’s screen

HeyMile works with Apple CarPlay. While you drive, the car’s screen shows the miles. When you park, two big buttons, Business and Personal, answer the drive with one tap.

While you drive

The car’s screen shows “Driving” and the miles so far, updated at most every 10 seconds, with two large buttons below: Business, a blue briefcase, and Personal, a green house. Tap one and the title shows your answer. Tap the other to change it. There is nothing to type and no list to scroll.

When you park

If the drive wasn’t answered on the way, the car’s screen shows the last drive, such as “Last drive: 22.8 mi”, with the same two buttons, so you can answer before you leave the car. The same question waits on your iPhone’s Lock Screen and in the widget.

The purpose is asked on your iPhone

What a business drive was for is never asked in the car. HeyMile asks on your iPhone: on the Lock Screen card, in a notification, or the next time you open the app.

Start every drive when your car connects

Besides detecting drives on its own, HeyMile can start a drive the moment your car connects, so every drive is caught from the start. Set it up by tapping the tracking status at the top of HeyMile’s first screen.

Set it up

  1. In the iPhone’s Settings, open HeyMile, then Location, and choose Always, with Precise Location on.
  2. Check that the top of HeyMile’s first screen says Tracking on.
  3. For the question to appear on the car’s screen when you park, turn on Show in CarPlay in the iPhone’s Settings, under Notifications, then HeyMile.

HeyMile then appears on your car’s screen with your other CarPlay apps.

Questions

Does HeyMile need CarPlay?

No. HeyMile works on the iPhone alone: the Lock Screen and the widget ask the same question. CarPlay adds the two buttons on the car’s screen.

Do I type anything in the car?

No. On CarPlay you only tap Business or Personal. The purpose of a business drive is asked on your iPhone, never in the car.

How do I get the question on my car’s screen after I park?

Turn on Show in CarPlay in the iPhone’s Settings, under Notifications, then HeyMile.
