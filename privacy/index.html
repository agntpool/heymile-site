Privacy Policy

Effective November 21, 2026

HeyMile is built so that your mileage log never reaches us. There is no HeyMile account. Your log lives on your iPhone and, if you use iCloud, in your own private iCloud. The one thing we keep is a record that you accepted the Terms of Service.

The short version

  • We cannot see your trips, your location or your voice. They are never sent to us.
  • We keep one record: that the Terms of Service were accepted on your copy of HeyMile, and when. It holds no name, email, location or trips.
  • HeyMile contains no advertising, no analytics and no tracking, and no code from other companies.
  • Location is used on your iPhone to detect and measure your drives.
  • Some features use services from Apple, such as Maps and iCloud. Those are listed below.
  • You can export or delete everything yourself, at any time, inside the app.

Who we are

HeyMile is an iPhone app operated by AGNTPOOL, LLC (“we”, “us”). This policy covers the HeyMile app and this website, heymile.app.

What HeyMile handles on your iPhone

The app works with the following information. All of it is processed on your iPhone.

Location

If you turn on automatic tracking, HeyMile uses your iPhone’s precise location to notice when a drive begins, to record its route and distance, and to notice when it ends. To do this when the app is closed, it asks for “Always” location access.

  • Between drives it relies on low-power signals from iOS that the phone has moved.
  • Precise GPS runs while a drive is being recorded and stops when the drive ends.
  • The route of each recorded drive is saved with the trip so you can see it on a map.

If you do not turn on automatic tracking, HeyMile does not use your location.

Motion

During a drive, HeyMile asks your iPhone whether you are riding or walking, so it can close the drive as soon as you leave the car. Your iPhone works this out itself. HeyMile does not store it, and it is not sent anywhere.

Your trips

For each trip HeyMile stores the date and start time, the duration, the distance, the start and end addresses, the route if it was recorded automatically, the type of trip, and whatever you add: the purpose, a client name and notes. It also stores the odometer readings you enter.

Your voice and what you type

When you tap the microphone, your speech is turned into text on your iPhone. HeyMile requires on-device speech recognition and does not use it otherwise. The audio is not recorded, stored or sent anywhere.

What you say or type is read on your iPhone to fill in a trip, answer a question about your trips, or find a trip you want to change. On iPhones that support Apple Intelligence, this uses Apple’s on-device model, which runs on your iPhone and can read your log but cannot change it; every change waits for you to tap Yes. The conversation itself is not saved; only the resulting trip is.

Settings

Your choices in the app, such as whether tracking and reminders are on, are stored on your iPhone, along with whether your subscription is active, the addresses of places you have named (such as “the office”), and a copy of any trip you deleted in the last 30 days.

Notifications and the Lock Screen

If you allow them, HeyMile shows reminders and a notice when a drive has been logged. These are created on your iPhone; we do not send them from a server. While a drive is being recorded, iOS can show its distance on the Lock Screen, in the Dynamic Island and, if your iPhone is connected to CarPlay, on the car’s screen. Afterwards the Lock Screen or the car’s screen can ask whether the trip was business or personal; the purpose is asked only on your iPhone. Anyone who can see these screens can see what they show.

What we collect

One thing: a record that you accepted the Terms of Service. When you tick the box on HeyMile’s welcome screen, and again if the terms change and you accept the new version, HeyMile saves:

  • the version of the terms, and the date and time you accepted it;
  • the sentence you agreed to;
  • the version of the app and of iOS;
  • a random identifier created for that installation of HeyMile;
  • if you subscribe, the transaction number Apple gives your subscription.

Apple’s iCloud, where the record is kept, adds the time it received the record and an anonymous identifier for the iCloud account that sent it.

The record holds no name, email address, phone number, location, trips, or anything you say or type. We keep it so that we can show the terms were accepted, and we use it for nothing else. It can be connected to you only if you give us the reference shown in HeyMile’s Settings, or the order number on your App Store receipt, for example in a dispute.

Nothing else. HeyMile does not send your location, trips, voice, contacts or usage to us or to anyone acting for us. There is no HeyMile account, so we do not hold your name, email address or password.

Apple may give us combined statistics about downloads, sales and crashes. These do not identify you, and crash and usage statistics are included only if you have chosen to share analytics with app developers in your iPhone’s settings.

Where your data is stored

  • On your iPhone. Your log is kept in the app’s own storage on your device.
  • In your private iCloud. If you are signed in to iCloud, your log is also kept in your private iCloud database, so it is backed up and returns when you set up a new iPhone. That database belongs to your Apple Account. We have no access to it.
  • The agreement record. Kept in a database in Apple’s iCloud that belongs to HeyMile and is separate from your private iCloud. Only we can read it. If your iPhone is not signed in to iCloud, the record waits on your iPhone until it is.
  • In your iPhone’s backups. Like other apps, HeyMile’s data may be included in the backups your iPhone makes to iCloud or to a computer.

Services from Apple that HeyMile uses

HeyMile uses only services provided by Apple. When it does, information goes to Apple, not to us, and Apple handles it under Apple’s Privacy Policy.

  • Apple Maps. To name the places where a drive started and ended, the app sends those two locations to Apple’s Maps service. If you say or type an address or the name of a place, such as a store or a mall, the app sends it to Apple Maps to find it and to measure the driving distance; to find places near you, it also sends the approximate area your iPhone is in. If a recorded drive starts somewhere other than where the last one ended, the app sends those two points to Apple Maps to measure the drive you may have missed, and asks you before adding it. When you look at a map in the app, Apple Maps loads the map for the area shown.
  • iCloud. Used to back up and sync your log, and to hold the agreement record, as described above.
  • The App Store. Your subscription is bought and billed through Apple. The app asks Apple whether your subscription is active. We never receive your payment details.
  • Siri and Shortcuts. If you use HeyMile through Siri, Shortcuts or the Action button, Apple processes the request and HeyMile carries it out on your iPhone.
  • Spotlight, widgets and controls. So you can search for a trip, HeyMile adds your trips to Spotlight’s index on your iPhone. The widget and the Drive control read a short summary that HeyMile keeps on your iPhone: your miles this year and the drive waiting for an answer. None of this leaves your iPhone.
  • Apple Intelligence and speech recognition. Both run on your device, as described above.

Sharing

We do not sell, rent or share your information. Your log never reaches us. The agreement record is disclosed only where needed to establish or defend a legal claim, or where the law requires it.

Reports leave your iPhone only when you choose to share one. A report contains the trips in the period you chose, including addresses, purposes and client names, so share it only with people you intend to.

Keeping and deleting your data

Your log stays until you delete it. You are in control of that:

  • Delete a single trip from the Trips list. A copy stays in Recently Deleted on that iPhone for 30 days, where you can restore it or remove it at once.
  • Delete everything by opening Settings in HeyMile and choosing Delete all my data. This removes your trips, your odometer readings and any report files the app created. If you use iCloud, the deletion is sent to your iCloud as well.
  • Deleting the app removes its data from that iPhone. A copy in iCloud remains until you delete it in the app, or in the iPhone’s Settings under your name, iCloud, Manage Storage.

The agreement record is not part of your log, and Delete all my data does not remove it. We keep it for as long as it may be needed to show that the terms were accepted, including after you stop using HeyMile.

Your choices

Each permission is optional and can be changed at any time in the iPhone’s Settings, under Apps, then HeyMile:

  • Location. Needed only for automatic tracking. Without it you can still log trips by voice or by typing.
  • Microphone and Speech Recognition. Needed only to speak to HeyMile. You can type instead.
  • Motion & Fitness. Used only to notice that you have left the car. Without it, a drive is closed after about three minutes without moving.
  • Notifications and Live Activities. Needed only for reminders and for showing a drive on the Lock Screen.
  • iCloud. If you turn off iCloud for HeyMile, your log stays on that iPhone only.

Security

Because your log is never sent to us, there is no database of logs that could be breached. The agreement record holds no personal details. Your data is protected by the security Apple builds into iPhone and iCloud, including your passcode and your Apple Account. Keeping those secure keeps your log secure.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, the law may give you rights to see, correct, export or delete your personal information. With HeyMile you can do all of these yourself, in the app: view and edit any trip, export your log as a PDF or a spreadsheet from Reports, and delete it as described above.

Because we do not hold your log, we cannot look up, send or delete it for you. To ask about the agreement record for your copy of HeyMile, email us with the reference shown at the bottom of HeyMile’s Settings. If you have a question about your privacy, contact us and we will help.

Children

HeyMile is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

This website

We do not use cookies, analytics or advertising trackers on heymile.app, and we do not build profiles of visitors. Every page and image comes from heymile.app itself; the site loads nothing from other companies.

Links from this site to the App Store carry a campaign label naming the page the link is on, such as “home”. When someone downloads HeyMile through one of these links, Apple reports to us only the total number of downloads for each label. We do not receive anything that identifies the person.

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, a service of GitHub, Inc. To deliver each page, GitHub’s servers receive the request your browser sends, which includes your IP address, the time, the page requested and your browser type. GitHub logs visitors’ IP addresses to keep the service secure. We do not receive these logs as a record of who you are, and we do not combine them with anything else. GitHub handles this information under the GitHub General Privacy Statement.

If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you choose to write, and we use it only to reply.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date. If a change affects how your data is handled, we will also say so in the app.
