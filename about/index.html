About HeyMile

Last updated November 21, 2026.

HeyMile (“HeyMile: Mileage Tracker” on the App Store) is a mileage log for iPhone and CarPlay, made by AGNTPOOL, LLC, a company in Texas, USA. It is for self-employed people, 1099 contractors and small businesses who drive for work.

Try it free

First month free for new subscribers, then $0.99 a month until you cancel. For iPhone with iOS 26 or later.

What it does

  • Detects drives and logs the miles, even with the app closed, once location access for HeyMile is set to Always and tracking is turned on in the app. It can also start a drive when the car connects.
  • Asks “Business or personal?” on the Lock Screen, in a Home Screen widget and on CarPlay, where two large buttons answer with one tap. A business drive then gets its purpose, asked on the iPhone.
  • Logs trips by voice or typing, such as “From the office to 1200 Main St”, measuring the miles with Apple Maps. It answers questions like “How many miles this month?” and changes a trip only after you say Yes.
  • Exports reports as a PDF or a spreadsheet for a month or a year, with each trip’s date, route, purpose and miles, and the odometer readings. Reports are generated in English.
  • Shows every trip on one map, business in blue and personal in green.

What it isn’t

  • Not tax advice. HeyMile is a record-keeping tool; its dollar figures are estimates using the IRS standard mileage rate and aren’t guaranteed.
  • Not for Android, iPad or the web: HeyMile is an iPhone app.
  • Not a team or fleet dashboard: each driver uses HeyMile on their own iPhone, with their own log.
  • Not connected to any rideshare, delivery or booking app. It logs the drives the iPhone detects.

Privacy

No account, no ads, no analytics. Trips stay on the iPhone and in the person’s own iCloud; we never see trips, locations or voice. The Privacy Policy explains it in full.

Choosing a mileage tracker

Things worth checking in any mileage app: whether it logs drives on its own, how you mark a drive business or personal, whether it works on CarPlay, whether it needs an account, where your trips are stored, what the report contains, and the price.

HeyMile fits if you use an iPhone, want one tap per drive and don’t want an account. Look elsewhere if you need Android, a shared team or fleet dashboard, or reports in a language other than English.
