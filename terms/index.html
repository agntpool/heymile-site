2. What HeyMile is

HeyMile is an iPhone app for keeping a log of the miles you drive. It can detect and record drives automatically, lets you add or describe trips by voice or by typing, and produces reports you can export.

HeyMile is built around United States mileage rules and the IRS standard mileage rates. It may not fit the rules of other countries.

HeyMile keeps your log on your iPhone and in your own iCloud. There is no HeyMile account. How your information is handled is described in the Privacy Policy.

4. Using automatic tracking responsibly

  • Use HeyMile to record your own driving, on your own iPhone. Do not use it to track another person.
  • Do not interact with the app while you are driving. Use your voice, or wait until you are parked. Follow the traffic laws where you drive; how you use your phone in the car is your responsibility.
  • Automatic tracking needs “Always” location access, and it uses battery while a drive is being recorded. You can turn it off at any time.
  • Do not use HeyMile to create records you know to be false, to break the law, or to copy, modify or reverse engineer the app.

5. Accuracy

Distances and addresses come from your iPhone’s location services and from Apple Maps. They are estimates. Signal, battery settings, permissions and how iOS manages apps in the background can all affect them. A drive may start recording late, end early, be missed, or include travel that was not a drive, such as a ride as a passenger.

You are responsible for reviewing your log, and for correcting, adding or deleting trips so that it is accurate. HeyMile does not promise that every drive will be recorded or that any distance is exact. Totals based on odometer readings depend on the readings you enter.
