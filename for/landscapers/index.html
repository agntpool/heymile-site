A route, every day

Landscaping and lawn care run on routes: a string of properties on the same day each week, plus estimates for new clients, the nursery or the landscape supply yard, small-engine repair, and runs to drop off green waste. Busy months mean long days, and the slow season brings different work and different drives.

Short drives add up

Each stop on a route is a short drive, and short drives are the easiest to forget. Over a season they add up to a lot of miles. The IRS expects a record of each business drive, with its date, place, purpose and miles, kept at or near the time (IRS Publication 463). HeyMile logs each stop it detects.

How HeyMile fits a route

  • Every stop, logged. HeyMile detects each drive and logs the miles, even with the app closed. Or have it start when your truck connects.
  • CarPlay in the truck. Business or Personal with one tap on the car’s screen, before you unload.
  • In English or Spanish. HeyMile follows your iPhone’s language, typed or spoken, so everyone on your team can use it in the language they prefer.
  • Business, then the purpose. Tap Business and HeyMile asks for the purpose.
  • Reports at the end of the season. PDF or spreadsheet, month or year.

Before your first drive: in the iPhone’s Settings, tap Apps, then HeyMile, then Location, and choose Always, with Precise Location on. Then check that the top of HeyMile’s first screen says Tracking on. Without both, HeyMile can’t log drives on its own.

Questions

Does HeyMile work in Spanish?

Yes. To use Spanish for HeyMile only, open the iPhone’s Settings, then Apps, HeyMile, Language. Reports are in English.

Several of us drive for the business. How does that work?

HeyMile logs the drives of the iPhone it’s on. Each driver uses HeyMile on their own iPhone, with their own log and their own report.

Can I change a drive to personal later?

Yes. Tell HeyMile “Make Monday’s trip personal”, or open the trip in Trips and change it.
