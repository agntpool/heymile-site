HeyMile for house cleaners

A mileage log for house cleaners.

Two or three homes a day, a stop for supplies, a new client’s walkthrough. If you clean homes or offices on your own or run a small cleaning business, HeyMile logs the drives between them.

A day spread across town

Cleaning work happens at the client’s door: a regular weekly home in the morning, an office after it closes, a move-out cleaning across town, an estimate visit for a new client, and the store for supplies in between. Each is a drive in your own car, and in a busy week there are dozens of them.

Many cleaners also drive their own errands between jobs, and HeyMile asks after each drive whether it was business or personal.

What the IRS publishes

If you’re self-employed, the miles between work locations may be deductible, and the IRS expects a record of each business drive with its date, destination, purpose and miles, kept at or near the time (IRS Publication 463). Which miles count is a question for your tax advisor.

How HeyMile fits your day

  • Every client visit, logged. HeyMile detects each drive and logs the miles, even with the app closed.
  • One tap between homes. When you park, the Lock Screen asks “Business or personal?” Tap Business and HeyMile asks for the purpose.
  • In English or Spanish. HeyMile follows your iPhone’s language, typed or spoken.
  • Add a drive by voice. Say “From the supply store to 1200 Main St” and HeyMile measures it with Apple Maps, then asks before it logs it.
  • A report for tax time. A PDF or a spreadsheet for the month or the year, with business and personal miles kept apart.

Before your first drive: in the iPhone’s Settings, tap Apps, then HeyMile, then Location, and choose Always, with Precise Location on. Then check that the top of HeyMile’s first screen says Tracking on. Without both, HeyMile can’t log drives on its own.

Questions

Does HeyMile work in Spanish?

Yes, typed or spoken. Reports are generated in English.

My team drives to jobs too. Can they use it?

Each person uses HeyMile on their own iPhone, with their own log and report. HeyMile has no shared team dashboard.

Can I write which client a drive was for?

Yes. Each business drive has a purpose, and you type it yourself.
