Long days, many clients

Independent couriers often work for several dispatchers or brokers, or directly for local businesses: a lab pickup in the morning, a court filing at noon, a parcel route in the afternoon. The days are long, and the paperwork comes from many places, each covering only its own jobs.

One log for all of it

As a contractor using your own vehicle, your mileage log is your record, not any one client’s. The IRS expects it to show each business drive’s date, destination, purpose and miles, kept at or near the time (IRS Publication 463), and Schedule C asks whether you have written evidence for the miles you claim.

HeyMile keeps the drives for every client in one log.

How HeyMile handles a courier’s day

  • Long days, logged. HeyMile detects your drives and logs the miles, even with the app closed.
  • One tap on CarPlay. Business or Personal on the car’s screen, at each stop.
  • A purpose for each drive. After you tap Business, HeyMile asks for the purpose.
  • Ask your log. “How many miles this month?” HeyMile answers from your log.
  • Monthly reports. Export a PDF or a spreadsheet and see every trip of the year on one map.

Before your first drive: in the iPhone’s Settings, tap Apps, then HeyMile, then Location, and choose Always, with Precise Location on. Then check that the top of HeyMile’s first screen says Tracking on. Without both, HeyMile can’t log drives on its own.

Questions

Can I keep one log for every client I drive for?

Yes. HeyMile logs your drives, not your clients’ jobs, so every run goes into one log. The purpose of each drive can name the client.

What if my phone missed a drive?

Tell HeyMile where you drove, for example “From the lab to 400 Oak Ave”. It measures the miles with Apple Maps and asks you to confirm.

Can I get a report for just one month?

Yes. In Reports, choose the month or the year, then view or share the PDF or spreadsheet.
