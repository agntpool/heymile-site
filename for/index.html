A mileage log for everyone who drives for work.

If you’re self-employed, a 1099 contractor or run a small business, the miles you drive for work may be deductible, but only if there’s a record. Find your kind of work below.

What every driver gets

  • Automatic logging. HeyMile detects drives and logs the miles, even with the app closed, or starts when your car connects.
  • One tap on the Lock Screen. When a drive ends, “Business or personal?” One tap answers, and a business drive gets its purpose.
  • CarPlay. Two big buttons on the car’s screen, Business and Personal, so the drive is answered before you leave the car.
  • Voice and typing. Say where you drove and HeyMile measures it with Apple Maps. Ask about your miles, or fix a trip. It always asks before it changes anything.
  • Reports. A PDF or a spreadsheet for the month or the year, with each trip’s date, route, purpose and miles, and your odometer readings.
  • Private. No account, no ads, no analytics. Your log lives on your iPhone and in your own iCloud.
