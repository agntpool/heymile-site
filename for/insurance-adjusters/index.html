Where the claims are

Field work goes wherever the losses are: a roof inspection in the morning, a vehicle at a body shop after lunch, a re-inspection across the county. After a storm, independent adjusters may work far from home for weeks, driving unfamiliar roads between dozens of properties.

Records made at the time

Adjusters know the value of documentation. The IRS expects the same of a mileage log: each business drive’s date, destination, purpose and miles, kept at or near the time (IRS Publication 463). Whether your miles are reimbursed by a firm or claimed on your own return, the log should be complete.

How HeyMile works in the field

  • Every inspection drive. HeyMile detects drives and logs the miles, wherever you take your iPhone.
  • A purpose for each. Tap Business, then note the inspection in a few words your firm allows.
  • Log by address. Say “From the hotel to 2200 Ridge Road”: HeyMile measures the miles with Apple Maps and asks before logging.
  • Find trips fast. Search Spotlight by place or purpose to find every drive to a property.
  • Reports and a map. Export the month as a PDF or a spreadsheet, and see every trip on one map.

Before your first drive: in the iPhone’s Settings, tap Apps, then HeyMile, then Location, and choose Always, with Precise Location on. Then check that the top of HeyMile’s first screen says Tracking on. Without both, HeyMile can’t log drives on its own.

Questions

Can I note the claim for each drive?

Yes, as the drive’s purpose. Keep it to what your firm allows in a mileage log.

Does HeyMile work when I’m deployed away from home?

Yes. HeyMile logs drives wherever you take your iPhone, and your log stays on it and in your own iCloud.

Can I send a report to the firm?

Yes. Export the month or the year as a PDF or a spreadsheet and share it from your iPhone.
