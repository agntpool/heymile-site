Life in a territory

Outside sales means driving a territory: scheduled calls, drop-in visits, sample deliveries, a trade show two cities over. Some reps are independent, paid on commission and responsible for their own costs. Others are employees reimbursed by the mile.

Reimbursement and the IRS

Companies that reimburse mileage usually ask for each trip’s date, destination, purpose and miles. IRS Publication 463 describes the same details, recorded at or near the time, for business miles claimed on a tax return (IRS Publication 463).

HeyMile logs the drives it detects as you go, so the month’s report is ready when you open Reports.

How HeyMile works on the road

  • Logs every call. HeyMile detects drives and logs the miles, so the visit you added at the last minute is there too.
  • Purpose in one answer. Tap Business and HeyMile asks what the drive was for.
  • Ask your log. “How many miles this month?” or “What trips did I take Monday?” HeyMile answers from your log.
  • Ready for the expense report. Export the month as a PDF or a spreadsheet and attach it.
  • CarPlay in the car. Business or Personal with one tap on the car’s screen, between appointments.

Before your first drive: in the iPhone’s Settings, tap Apps, then HeyMile, then Location, and choose Always, with Precise Location on. Then check that the top of HeyMile’s first screen says Tracking on. Without both, HeyMile can’t log drives on its own.

Questions

Can HeyMile make a monthly report for my expense report?

Yes. Open Reports, choose the month, and export a PDF or a spreadsheet with each trip’s date, route, purpose and miles.

Can I see how many miles I drove this month?

Yes. Ask HeyMile “How many miles this month?”, or open Reports.

Does my company see my log?

No. HeyMile has no account, and nothing is shared unless you export a report and send it yourself.
