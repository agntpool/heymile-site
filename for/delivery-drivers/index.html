What a delivery shift looks like

Food and grocery delivery means short hops between restaurants, stores and doorsteps, waiting in parking lots, and driving back toward busier areas between orders. A single evening can be twenty drives or more, and a weekend can mix delivery runs with your own errands in the same car.

Few people write down twenty drives a night by hand. Rebuilding them months later from memory and order history is slow, and it leaves gaps.

Why a log made as you drive matters

A mileage log is most useful when it’s made as you go. The IRS describes adequate records as ones made at or near the time of the drive, showing the date, the place, the business purpose and the miles (IRS Publication 463). A delivery app’s summary covers only its own orders, counted its own way.

Mixed days are where a log earns its keep: the drive to the store for an order is business, the stop for your own groceries isn’t. One tap at the end of each drive keeps them apart.

How HeyMile handles a delivery shift

  • Logs in the background. HeyMile detects drives even when it’s closed, so your delivery app can stay on screen.
  • Business or Personal on CarPlay. Two big buttons on the car’s screen answer each drive with one tap, before you head to the door.
  • A widget that asks for you. On the Home Screen, the widget shows your miles this year and, when a drive is waiting, Business and Personal in one tap.
  • Start a drive yourself. Add the Drive control to Control Center or the Action button to start or end a drive with one press.
  • One report for every app. Export the month or the year as a PDF or a spreadsheet, with business and personal miles kept apart.

Before your first drive: in the iPhone’s Settings, open HeyMile, then Location, and choose Always, with Precise Location on. Then check that the top of HeyMile’s first screen says Tracking on. Without both, HeyMile can’t log drives on its own.

Questions

Will HeyMile keep logging while my delivery app is open?

Yes. HeyMile detects drives in the background, even when it’s closed, so your delivery app can stay on screen.

I deliver for several apps. Do I need separate logs?

No. HeyMile logs your drives, whichever app sent you, so all your delivery miles are in one log and one report.

Can I keep my own errands out of it?

Yes. Every drive is Business or Personal. Personal drives stay in your log, but reports list them apart from your business miles.
